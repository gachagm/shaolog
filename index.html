The Theory

Rishu is the only consort without a clearly named gemstone. Many fans believe she may represent a pearl — but it is never confirmed.

Perhaps this is intentional. While others are defined by jewels, Rishu stands apart.

As "the virtuous one", she may symbolize humility — a beauty that does not need to shine to be valuable.
